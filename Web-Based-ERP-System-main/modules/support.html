Frequentlyaskedquestions

Get quick answers to common questions about procurement.

What can I see on the Dashboard?

The Dashboard summarizes your procurement activity, including pending transactions and recent request or order updates.

Can I track pending transactions?

Yes. Review pending items from the Dashboard or open the related Purchase Request or Purchase Order to check its current status.

How do I review a PR?

Open Purchase Request and select a request awaiting review. Check its item details, quantities, reference information, and supporting notes before deciding.

Can I approve or reject a PR?

Open a pending request and choose the appropriate review action. If you reject it, include a clear reason so the requester knows what needs to change.

What if a support concern is unresolved?

Use Contact support to describe the issue and include its ticket or procurement reference. You can check for updates in Recent support requests.

Can I view resolved concerns?

Resolved concerns appear in the support request history with a Resolved status. Use the request details to review the recorded subject and category.

What happens after PR approval?

Approval advances the purchase through your procurement workflow. Follow the request and its related Purchase Order for the next status updates.

How do I track a PO?

Open Purchase Order to review its status and details. If the order is associated with a container, check Containers for its shipment information.

Can I update product details?

Open the relevant procurement record and check which details are editable. If the information is locked or needs correction, contact support with the record reference.

How do I generate reports?

Open Reports, choose the report you need, and set its available filters before reviewing or exporting the results.

What if I encounter an error?

Retry the action and note what you were doing if the error continues. Contact support with the affected record reference and a screenshot if possible.

How do I track my concerns?

Check Recent support requests for the ticket status. Open a request to review its subject, category, and latest recorded status.
