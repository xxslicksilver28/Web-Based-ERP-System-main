Frequentlyaskedquestions

Get quick answers to common questions about procurement.

How do I get an account?

Ask your workspace manager to create an account or invite you to the procurement workspace.

Why change my password?

Keep your account secure by using a unique password. If you need help updating your sign-in details, contact your workspace manager.

Why can't I register directly?

Accounts are managed by your organization so access stays limited to approved team members. Contact your workspace manager for access.

How to create a PR?

Open Purchase Request, add the items and quantities you need, then submit the request for review.

What if my PR is rejected?

Review the rejection notes, update the request if needed, and submit it again for approval.

What is an RFQ?

A request for quotation asks suppliers to provide pricing and terms for the items you need.

How do I compare quotations?

Compare each supplier's item pricing, quantities, delivery estimates, and terms before selecting an offer.

What is a Purchase Order?

A Purchase Order records the approved items, quantities, supplier, and terms for a purchase.

Can I edit a sent PO?

Contact the person managing the order before making changes to a Purchase Order that has already been sent.

Why can't I order extra quantities?

Order quantities may be limited to what was approved. Request an amendment if you need additional items.

What is Goods Receiving?

Goods Receiving records the items delivered against a Purchase Order so the received quantities can be checked.

What follows receipt confirmation?

After confirming receipt, keep the delivery record with the order and follow your organization's next invoice or payment steps.
